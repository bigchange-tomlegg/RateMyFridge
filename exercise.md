# 🥬 Rate My Fridge

## The Challenge

You have **45 mins** to vibe-code and ship a mobile app that answers one important question:

> **“How good is my fridge?”**

Users take a photo of their fridge. The app analyses it and gives them a brutally honest, entertaining assessment.

There are **two teams**:

* 🍎 **Team iOS**
* 🤖 **Team Android**

Both teams have access to the same AI backend and the same core challenge.

Your job is to build the **best mobile experience** around it.

---

## 🎯 The Goal

By the end of the session, we want a **working app running on a real phone**.

It doesn't need to be production-ready.

It does need to be:

* Fun
* Fast
* Surprisingly polished
* Something you'd actually want to show someone
* Something that makes people say **“you built that in 45 mins?”**

This is a **vibe-coding challenge**, so use whatever tools, AI assistants, libraries and shortcuts help you move quickly.

---

# 🧊 The Core Experience

The basic journey should be:

### 1. Open the app

Make the user immediately understand what it does.

> **RATE MY FRIDGE**
> Let's see what you're working with.

### 2. Take a photo

Use the phone camera to capture the inside of the fridge.

### 3. Analyse

Send the image to the shared Rate My Fridge API.

While waiting, have some fun.

Examples:

> 🔍 Inspecting your questionable food choices...

> 🥒 Detecting vegetables...

> 🚨 Looking for signs of a functioning adult...

### 4. Get your verdict

The API returns:

* Score /100
* Funny verdict
* Items detected
* Advice
* Fridge “vibe”

Turn this into a **great result screen**.

Example:

> # 37/100
>
> **Your fridge needs professional help.**
>
> 🥒 Half a cucumber
> 🫙 7 sauces
> 🧀 Something that may once have been cheese
>
> **Vibe:** CHAOS
>
> *“You have enough condiments to survive a nuclear winter, but apparently no actual food.”*

### 5. Share it

Give users a reason to show someone else.

A screenshot/shareable result is highly encouraged.

### Polish

Are the tiny details surprisingly good?


---

# 🚫 Don't Waste Time On

You have 45 mins - 1hour.

Don't spend half of it building infrastructure.

You **do not need**:

* User accounts
* Authentication
* Payments
* A database
* A complex backend
* App Store / Play Store submission
* Perfect architecture
* Production-grade analytics
* An elaborate settings screen

The shared AI API is already provided.

**Your job is the mobile experience.**
